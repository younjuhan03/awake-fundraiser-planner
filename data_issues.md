# Data issues log

Every inconsistency or gap found while building the raw CSVs, and how it was handled. Nothing was silently fixed.
Source data was transcribed from screenshots (account book, planning memo, pre-order form sheet); the pre-order form
was anonymized before transcription (no names or phone numbers).

**Initial verification (all matched, no mismatches):** 29 orders, 104 items, $375 (S26); F24 costs $261.46;
S25 costs $190.00 and 108 items. Re-checked on every build by `awake.analysis.verify_totals` and `tests/test_data.py`.

| # | Issue | Where | How it was handled |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **F24 net profit: ledger memo says $261.13, transactions give $234.54** (revenue $496.00 − fundraiser expenses $261.46). Unexplained difference: $26.59. | Account book, F24 | Unresolved. Both reported everywhere. **$234.54 is the primary figure** because it is reproducible from the transactions. Unverified observation: the memo figure is within $0.33 of the *expense* total ($261.46), so the memo may have quoted a different quantity; this was not confirmed and nothing was changed on that guess. Ask the 2024–25 finance leader. |
